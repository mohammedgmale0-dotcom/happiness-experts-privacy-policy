Happiness Experts Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 15, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Happiness Experts handles information in connection with its website, marketing tools, and Pinterest API integration.

About Happiness Experts

Happiness Experts is a print-on-demand brand. We use digital tools to research marketing opportunities, prepare and manage organic marketing content, and promote our existing products through authorized channels.

Information We Collect

Happiness Experts may receive information that you voluntarily provide when you contact us or interact with services we operate.

We do not request or collect Pinterest passwords or Pinterest session cookies.

Pinterest API Integration

Happiness Experts may use the official Pinterest API to connect our authorized Pinterest account with our marketing workflow.

The integration is intended to help manage authorized organic Pinterest activities for the Happiness Experts account, including working with Pins, Boards, content, links, and permitted account or performance information where authorized by Pinterest.

Pinterest account access is performed only through Pinterest-authorized authentication and permissions. We do not ask users to provide their Pinterest passwords to Happiness Experts.

How We Use Information

Information is used only as necessary to operate and improve our services, manage authorized marketing activities, understand performance, maintain security, and communicate when appropriate.

Pinterest Data

Information accessed through Pinterest is handled in accordance with Pinterest's Developer Guidelines, API terms, applicable permissions, and applicable law.

Happiness Experts does not sell Pinterest API information or share Pinterest API information with third parties for unrelated advertising or data-broker purposes.

Automation and User Control

Happiness Experts may use approved automation and marketing tools to assist with research, content preparation, and authorized marketing workflows. Pinterest actions are intended to operate only within the permissions and authorization granted for the connected Happiness Experts Pinterest account and in accordance with Pinterest's applicable policies.

Third-Party Services

Happiness Experts may use third-party service providers and platforms necessary to operate its website and authorized marketing workflow, including Pinterest and technology or hosting providers. Their handling of information is governed by their own applicable terms and privacy policies.

Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, to maintain security, or to comply with applicable legal obligations. Pinterest information is not retained where retention is prohibited by Pinterest's applicable developer terms or policies.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information and credentials used in connection with our services. API credentials and access tokens are treated as confidential and are not intentionally disclosed publicly.

Your Privacy Requests

You may contact Happiness Experts regarding questions about this Privacy Policy or requests concerning personal information associated with our services.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our services, integrations, or legal obligations change. The latest version will be published on this page with an updated revision date.
