About Happiness Experts

Happiness Experts is a print-on-demand brand offering products featuring motivational, positive, and lifestyle designs.

Information We Collect

Happiness Experts may process information necessary to operate its services and authorized integrations. This may include information provided directly by users and information made available through authorized third-party APIs.

Pinterest API Integration

Happiness Experts intends to use the official Pinterest API to manage authorized Pinterest content for the Happiness Experts account, including reading permitted account, board, Pin, and analytics information and creating or managing authorized organic content where Pinterest permissions allow it.

Happiness Experts does not request or collect Pinterest passwords or session cookies. Pinterest access is performed only through Pinterest's authorized authentication and permission mechanisms.

How We Use Information

Information is used only to operate, improve, analyze, and manage authorized Happiness Experts marketing activities and integrations.

How Information Is Stored and Shared

Information is retained only as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy. Happiness Experts does not sell Pinterest API data or share it with data brokers or for unrelated advertising purposes. Information may be processed by service providers used to operate the business and its technical integrations when necessary.

Data Security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures are used to protect information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, or disclosure.

Third-Party Services
